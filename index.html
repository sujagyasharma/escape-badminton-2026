About the tournament

Four days of badminton for Escape

A tournament for Escape residents, of every age, to play together and get to know each other on court.

CamaraderieMeet neighbours across towers and age groups.
FitnessA reason to get moving, from under-10s to over-60s.
SkillsCompetitive matches to sharpen your game.
SportsmanshipPlay hard, play fair, shake hands at the net.
